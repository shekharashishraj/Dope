\section*{Multiple Choice (10 marks)}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\arabic*.]
\item A man is charged with murder. During the trial, defense counsel offers the testimony of a man on death row admitting he hired another man to shoot the victim. The testimony is
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item not hearsay.
    \item hearsay, but admissible as an admission.
    \item hearsay, but admissible as a dying declaration.
    \item hearsay not within any recognized exception.
\end{enumerate}
\item In most states, the division of homicide into degrees is distinguished according to which of the following?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item the causal relationship between the defendant's act and the resulting death.
    \item the attendant circumstances surrounding the death.
    \item the nature of the act causing the death.
    \item the defendant's state of mind at the time the killing was committed.
\end{enumerate}
\item A defendant charged with first-degree murder shall be furnished with a list containing names and addresses of all prospective jurors
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item upon court order.
    \item upon request.
    \item upon request and showing of good cause.
    \item under no circumstances.
\end{enumerate}
\item Under Article III, federal judicial power extends to which of the following kinds of cases?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item interpretation of treaties
    \item interpretation of admiralty laws
    \item disputes between states and foreign citizens
    \item all of the above
\end{enumerate}
\item A criminal actor committed the crime of solicitation. Which of the following does not apply to solicitation?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item It is an agreement between two or more to commit a crime.
    \item It is solicitation of another to commit a felony.
    \item No act is needed other than the solicitation.
    \item Withdrawal is generally not a defense.
\end{enumerate}
\end{enumerate}
