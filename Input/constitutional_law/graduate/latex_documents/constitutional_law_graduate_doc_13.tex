\section*{Multiple Choice (10 marks)}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\arabic*.]
\item Termination of a parent's custody rights and also termination of welfare benefits both require what type of process?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item Prior notice and prior evidentiary hearing
    \item Prior notice and opportunity to respond
    \item Prior screening by neutral fact finder
    \item Prior screening and subsequent evidentiary hearing
\end{enumerate}
\item There is a voting law that is going to be reviewed by the court. If the law is upheld, what must be true?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item The law is necessary to achieve a compelling government purpose.
    \item The law is substantially related to an important government purpose.
    \item The law is rationally related to a legitimate government purpose.
    \item The law is substantially related to a legitimate government purpose.
\end{enumerate}
\item Judge took judicial notice of some facts at the beginning of the trial. Which of the following is not an appropriate kind of fact for judicial notice?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item Indisputable facts.
    \item Facts that have been asserted by individual political organizations.
    \item Facts recognized to be true by common knowledge.
    \item Facts capable of scientific verification.
\end{enumerate}
\item Which is the hierarchy of U.S. Law?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item Executive agreements, U.S. Constitution, treaties and federal statutes, state law
    \item U.S constitution, treaties and federal statutes, executive agreements, state law
    \item Treaties and federal statutes, US Constitution, executive agreements, state law
    \item U.S constitution, executive agreements, treaties and federal statutes, state law
\end{enumerate}
\item In which situation would a Defendant not be held to the standard of care of a reasonable person?

    \item Defendant is deaf, and is accused of being negligent in failing to heed a warning bell.
    \item Defendant has poor judgment.
    \item Defendant is hot-tempered and damaged property in a tantrum.
    \item Defendant has an I.Q. of 85.
\end{enumerate}
\end{enumerate}
