\section*{Multiple Choice (10 marks)}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\arabic*.]
\item In which of the following situations does the best evidence rule generally not apply?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item Collateral matters
    \item Public records
    \item When it's not offered to prove the contents of the document
    \item All of the above.
\end{enumerate}
\item A criminal actor committed the crime of solicitation. Which of the following does not apply to solicitation?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item It is an agreement between two or more to commit a crime.
    \item It is solicitation of another to commit a felony.
    \item No act is needed other than the solicitation.
    \item Withdrawal is generally not a defense.
\end{enumerate}
\item In which of the following stages does an indigent person not have the Sixth Amendment right to counsel?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item Preliminary Hearing
    \item Arraignment
    \item investigative surveillance
    \item Post-charge lineups
\end{enumerate}
\item A man is charged with murder. During the trial, defense counsel offers the testimony of a man on death row admitting he hired another man to shoot the victim. The testimony is
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item not hearsay.
    \item hearsay, but admissible as an admission.
    \item hearsay, but admissible as a dying declaration.
    \item hearsay not within any recognized exception.
\end{enumerate}
\item Which of the following is not an element of common law burglary?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item A breaking and entry
    \item Of a dwelling, of another
    \item During the daytime
    \item With the intent to commit a felony therein.
\end{enumerate}
\end{enumerate}
