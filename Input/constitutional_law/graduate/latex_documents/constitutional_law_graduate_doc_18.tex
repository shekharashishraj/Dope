\section*{Multiple Choice (10 marks)}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\arabic*.]
\item In order to have an irrevocable offer under the Uniform Commercial Code, the offer must
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item Be made by a merchant to a merchant.
    \item Be contained in a signed writing which gives assurance that the offer will be held open.
    \item State the period of time for which it is irrevocable.
    \item Not be contained in a form supplied by the offeror.
\end{enumerate}
\item To prove a discriminatory classification, it must be shown that the government had intent to discriminate. Such intent can be shown by all but which of the following?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item A law that is discriminatory on its face.
    \item A discriminatory application of a facially neutral law.
    \item A discriminatory effect.
    \item A discriminatory motive.
\end{enumerate}
\item Which of the following will not terminate a contract by operation of law?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item Death or insanity of a party.
    \item Partial performance
    \item Destruction of the subject matter of contract.
    \item Supervening illegality
\end{enumerate}
\item If a regulation does amount to a taking, then what must the government do?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item Pay just compensation for the property
    \item Terminate the regulation and pay damages that occurred while regulation was in effect
    \item Pay double damages if not addressed in 30 days.
    \item a or b
\end{enumerate}
\item The most likely contract to be classified under the Uniform Commercial Code (UCC) is a contract for
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item A common carrier delivering a new computer.
    \item Crops and timber to be severed from the property next summer.
    \item The purchase of a commercial property building.
    \item The sale of an intangible asset.
\end{enumerate}
\end{enumerate}
