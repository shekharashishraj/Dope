\section*{Multiple Choice (10 marks)}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\arabic*.]
\item Which proposition below is the most consistent with what Rawls claims the POP would opt for in respect of 'social primary goods'?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item The POP will choose wealth over a compassionate society.
    \item The POP will choose equality over power.
    \item The POP will be unselfish.
    \item The POP will choose to protect the disabled.
\end{enumerate}
\item Tom and Jerry entered into a contract whereby Tom agreed to sell Jerry \$1,000 worth of heroin, an illegal substance. This is an example of a:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item quasi contract
    \item void contract
    \item voidable contract
    \item secondary party beneficiary contract
\end{enumerate}
\item Which proposition below is the most powerful refutation of Olivecrona's psychological explanation of law?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item Coercion plays no part in our decision to obey the law.
    \item He provides no empirical evidence in support of his thesis.
    \item His argument is itself metaphysical.
    \item He neglects the relation between morality and legal duty.
\end{enumerate}
\item Iverson Jewelers wrote a letter to Miller, 'We have received an exceptionally fine self winding Rolox watch which we will sell to you at a very favorable price.'
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item The letter is an offer to sell
    \item A valid offer cannot be made by letter.
    \item The letter contains a valid offer which will terminate within a reasonable time.
    \item The letter lacks one of the essential elements of an offer.
\end{enumerate}
\item What is the legal nature of the Universal Declaration of Human Rights (UDHR)?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item The UDHR is a multilateral treaty
    \item The UDHR is a UN General Assembly resolution
    \item The UDHR is a UN Security Council resolution
    \item The UDHR is a declaration adopted by several States at an international conference
\end{enumerate}
\end{enumerate}
