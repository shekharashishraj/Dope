\section*{Multiple Choice (10 marks)}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\arabic*.]
\item Which of the following events has no effect on GDP?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item A college buys computers.
    \item The Department of Transportation repaves a road.
    \item A city spends money to refurbish a public park.
    \item You buy a 1957 Chevy from a friend.
\end{enumerate}
\item A monopsonist is identified by one of the following:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item A wage payment lower than the average revenue product of labor.
    \item Employment level lower than that of a competitive labor market.
    \item A single buyer of labor services.
    \item Employment level greater than that of a competitive labor market.
    \item A wage payment lower than the marginal revenue product of labor.
\end{enumerate}
\item What is the immediate effect on the money supply if Bank A re-ceives a deposit of $ 1000 in currency from Mr. X and grants a loan of $ 500 to Mr. Y?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item add \$1000 to the money supply
    \item add \$ 500 to the money supply
    \item no change in the money supply
    \item reduce \$1000 from the money supply
\end{enumerate}
\item Households demand more money as an asset when
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item the demand for goods and services decreases.
    \item the stock market crashes.
    \item bond prices fall.
    \item the nominal interest rate falls.
    \item the unemployment rate rises.
\end{enumerate}
\item The government decides to levy a lump-sum tax which will reducedisposable income by \$50 billion. If the MPS = 0.25, whateffect will this have on NNP?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item \$50 billion
    \item \$87.5 billion
    \item \$100 billion
    \item \$37.5 billion
    \item \$200 billion
\end{enumerate}
\end{enumerate}
