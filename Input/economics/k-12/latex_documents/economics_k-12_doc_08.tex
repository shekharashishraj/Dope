\section*{Multiple Choice (10 marks)}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\arabic*.]
\item Suppose that the federal government reclassified the purchase of a new home as consumption spending rather than investment spending. This decision would
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item increase aggregate demand and decrease real GDP.
    \item decrease aggregate demand and decrease real GDP.
    \item decrease aggregate demand and increase real GDP.
    \item have no impact on aggregate demand and real GDP.

\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item M stands for the money supply and Q stands for quality.
    \item V stands for the velocity of GDP and Q stands for quality.

\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item the value of the dollar falls as the supply of dollars increases.
    \item the value of the dollar rises as demand for dollars increases.
    \item the value of the dollar falls as demand for dollars decreases.
    \item the value of the dollar rises as the supply of dollars increases.
\end{enumerate}
\item Which of the following is an implicit cost for the owner of a small store in your hometown?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]

    \item The cost of purchasing canned goods from a wholesale food distributor

    \item The cost of cooling the refrigerated meat display
\end{enumerate}

    \item faces a downward sloping demand curve
\end{enumerate}
\end{enumerate}
