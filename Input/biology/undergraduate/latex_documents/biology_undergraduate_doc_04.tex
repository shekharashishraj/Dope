\section*{Multiple Choice (10 marks)}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\arabic*.]
\item Which of the following is an incorrect statement about gel electrophoresis?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item DNA migrates from positive charge to negative charge.
    \item Smaller DNA travels faster.
    \item The DNA migrates only when the current is running.
    \item The longer the current is running, the farther the DNA will travel.
\end{enumerate}
\item During the period when life is believed to have begun, the atmosphere on primitive Earth contained abundant amounts of all the following gases EXCEPT
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item hydrogen
    \item methane
    \item nitrogen
    \item sulfur dioxide
    \item neon
\end{enumerate}
\item Mutation of homeotic cluster genes often results in which of the following developmental defects in Drosophila?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item Complete absence of segmentation
    \item Hindrance in the development of the nervous system
    \item Inability to undergo metamorphosis
    \item Absence of a group of contiguous segments
    \item Tumor formation in imaginal discs
\end{enumerate}
\item Which of the following would most likely provide examples of mitotic cell divisions?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item cross section of muscle tissue
    \item longitudinal section of a shoot tip
    \item longitudinal section of a leaf vein
    \item cross section of a fruit
    \item cross section of a leaf
\end{enumerate}
\item Vinegar is classified as an acid. What does this tell you about the concentration of the hydrogen ions and the hydroxide ions?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item Vinegar has a lower concentration of hydrogen ions than hydroxide ions
    \item Vinegar has an excess of hydrogen ions with respect to hydroxide ions
    \item Vinegar has a higher concentration of hydroxide ions than water
    \item Vinegar has no hydroxide ions
    \item Vinegar does not contain hydrogen ions
\end{enumerate}
\end{enumerate}
