\section*{Multiple Choice (10 marks)}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\arabic*.]
\item Occasionally a boy is born withundescendedtestes (cryptorchidism), and he will be sterile unless they are surgically caused to descend into the scrotum. Explain the reason for this.
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item Human sperm cannot develop at the high temperature found within the body cavity.
    \item The testes require the lower pressure environment of the scrotum for sperm maturation
    \item Testes must be in the scrotum to properly attach to the vas deferens
    \item The scrotum provides the necessary nutrients for sperm development
    \item Gravity is required for the testes to function properly
\end{enumerate}
\item Sea divers are aware of a danger known as the "bends". Explain the physiological mechanism of the bends.
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item Carbon dioxide bubbles forming in the blood causing acidosis
    \item Rapid increase in barometric pressure causing the bends
    \item Formation of helium bubbles in the bloodstream due to rapid ascent
    \item Increased salinity in the water leading to dehydration and joint pain
    \item Oxygen bubbles forming in the blood leading to hypoxia
\end{enumerate}
\item What is the function of the contractile vacuole?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item Protects the cell from foreign invaders
    \item Acts as a storage for genetic material
    \item Regulates the pH level within the cell
    \item Transports oxygen throughout the cell
    \item Removes nitrogenous wastes
\end{enumerate}
\item Why is genetic drift less important in human evolution at present than it was twenty thousand or more years ago?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item The human genome has fully adapted to current environmental pressures
    \item Modern medicine has eliminated the effects of genetic drift
    \item The rate of genetic drift is directly proportional to technological advancements
    \item Humans have reached a genetic equilibrium where drift is no longer possible
    \item Humans have developed immunity to genetic drift
\end{enumerate}
\item Chromosomal sex in mammals has been shown to be strongly Y chromosome determined. How was this shown to be true?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item The absence of a Y chromosome determines maleness in mammals
    \item The presence of a Y chromosome usually determines maleness in mammals
    \item The ratio of two X chromosomes to one Y chromosome determines maleness
    \item The presence of an X chromosome determines maleness in mammals
    \item The presence of an X chromosome in the absence of a Y chromosome determines maleness
\end{enumerate}
\end{enumerate}
