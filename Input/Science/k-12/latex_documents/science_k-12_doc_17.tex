\item How does acid rain most likely affect forests?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item Top soil loses all of its nutrients.
    \item Plants begin to grow deeper roots.
    \item Animals have more food sources.
    \item Trees become less healthy over time.

\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item They change chemical energy into kinetic energy.
    \item They change solar energy into chemical energy.
    \item They change wind energy into heat energy.
    \item They change mechanical energy into solar energy.

\item Two layers of sedimentary rock are exposed on the side of a hill. Only one of the layers has fossils. The lack of fossils in one layer of sedimentary rock is most likely due to
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item changing environments.
    \item volcanic activity.
    \item changing rates of erosion.
    \item evaporation of sea water.
\end{enumerate}
\item Although they belong to the same family, an eagle and a pelican are different. What is one difference between them?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]

    \item their method of reproduction
    \item their method of catching food
\end{enumerate}
\end{enumerate}
