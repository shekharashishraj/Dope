\section*{Multiple Choice (10 marks)}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\arabic*.]
\item A car is rolling on a level street. What can make the car stop moving?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item a force that pushes straight up
    \item a force that pushes straight down
    \item a force that pushes the same direction that the car is moving
    \item a force that pushes the opposite of the direction that the car is moving
\end{enumerate}
\item The human body produces motion by changing chemical energy into mechanical energy. Which of these best describes what happens to the energy?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item The total amount of energy increases.
    \item The total amount of energy is constant.
    \item The energy is destroyed through motion.
    \item The amount of chemical energy increases.
\end{enumerate}
\item A student walking through the forest notices moss growth increases as the density of the forest increases. The student has made
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item a hypothesis.
    \item a conclusion.
    \item an assumption.
    \item an observation.
\end{enumerate}
\item The gallbladder stores bile, a fluid that aids in digestion of fat. Which of the following types of food should be avoided by a person whose gallbladder has been removed?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item fruits
    \item grains
    \item cheese
    \item vegetables
\end{enumerate}
\item A strong magnet will separate a mixture of
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item clear glass and green glass.
    \item paper cups and plastic cups.
    \item iron nails and aluminum nails.
    \item sand and salt.
\end{enumerate}
\end{enumerate}
