\begin{enumerate}[label=\arabic*.]
\item Which step should a student do in order to be able to repeat an experiment?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item do the experiment many times
    \item keep a record of all procedures
    \item make a model of the set-up of the experiment
    \item change some of the conditions of the experiment

\item Leopard's opening chant suggests that he is
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item unhappy.
    \item confused.
    \item confident.
    \item generous.
\end{enumerate}
\item What do the energy resources uranium and coal have in common?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item They both are formed from the remains of dead organisms.
    \item They both are acquired by mining the lithosphere.
    \item They both release energy through combustion.
    \item They both generate radioactive wastes.
\end{enumerate}
\item Many different plant and animal species live in the Amazon rainforest. Which is the most likely result of large areas of deforestation in the Amazon rainforest?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item an increase in animal populations
    \item a decrease in oxygen production
    \item an increase in precipitation
    \item a decrease in pollutants
\end{enumerate}
\item Why does a town in the desert rarely experience early morning fog as compared to a town along the coast?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item There is less rainfall in the desert.
    \item Temperatures vary more in the desert.
    \item There is less water vapor in the desert air.
    \item There are fewer plants in the desert.
\end{enumerate}
\end{enumerate}
