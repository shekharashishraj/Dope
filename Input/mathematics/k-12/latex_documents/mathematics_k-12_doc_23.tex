\section*{Multiple Choice (10 marks)}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\arabic*.]
\item Gwen wrote the number pattern below on a piece of paper. 1, 5, 9, 13 What are the next two terms in Gwen's pattern?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item 15, 17
    \item 15, 19
    \item 17, 19
    \item 17, 21
\end{enumerate}
\item The Sojourn family went on a vacation. They started with $2000. If they spent $150 each day, which expression represents how much money they had after x days?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item 1850 x
    \item 2000 -150 x
    \item 150 x
    \item 2000 +150 x
\end{enumerate}
\item Simplify $\frac{k-3}{2} + 3 k+1+\frac{3 k+1}{2}$.
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item 3 k+1
    \item 5 k
    \item 3 k
    \item 1
\end{enumerate}
\item Which step explains how to find the value of a in 6 a = 72?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item add 6 to both sides
    \item divide both sides by 6
    \item multiply both sides by 6
    \item subtract 6 from both sides
\end{enumerate}
\item The rainfall for the last three months of school was 8.1 inches, 4.2 inches, and 0.33 inch. Estimate the total rainfall for the three months by rounding to the nearest whole number.
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item 11.9 in.
    \item 11 in.
    \item 45.0 in.
    \item 12 in.
\end{enumerate}
\end{enumerate}
