\section*{Multiple Choice (10 marks)}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\arabic*.]
\item The atomic mass of the 4 He atom is 4.002603 u. Find the binding energy of the 4 He nucleus in MeV.
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item 33.9
    \item 29.8
    \item 27.5
    \item 31.2
    \item 24.3
\end{enumerate}
\item Calculate the self-inductance of a coil if a rate of change of current of 5 amperes per second produces a backemfof 2 volts.
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item 0.4 henry
    \item 0.3 henry
    \item 0.2 henry
    \item 0.6 henry
    \item 0.7 henry
\end{enumerate}
\item What happens to the force of gravitational attraction between two small objects if the mass of each object is doubled and the distance between their centers is doubled?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item It is increased by two times.
    \item It remains the same.
    \item It becomes zero.
    \item It is reduced to half.
    \item It is increased by eight times.
\end{enumerate}
\item What is the focal length, of a combination of two thin convex lenses, each of focal length f, placed apart at a distance of (2 f / 3)?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item (f / 3)
    \item (f / 2)
    \item (5 f / 3)
    \item (6 f / 5)
    \item (3 f / 4)
\end{enumerate}
\item Why are the season's in Mars' southern hemisphere so extreme?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item because Mars' axis is less tilted than the Earth's
    \item because Mars has a smaller size than the Earth
    \item because Mars has a slower rotation speed than the Earth
    \item because Mars has more carbon dioxide in its atmosphere than the Earth
    \item because Mars has a shorter year than the Earth
\end{enumerate}
\end{enumerate}
