\section*{Multiple Choice (10 marks)}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\arabic*.]
\item Which of the following characteristics would not necessarily suggest that a rock we found is a meteorite.
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item It has a fusion crust
    \item It contains solidified spherical droplets
    \item It is highly processed
    \item It has different elemental composition than earth
\end{enumerate}
\item What would weigh the most on the moon?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item A kilogram of feathers
    \item Five pounds of bricks as measured on Earth
    \item Five kilograms of feathers
    \item A kilogram of bricks
\end{enumerate}
\item Which of the following most likely explains why Venus does not have a strong magnetic field?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item Its rotation is too slow.
    \item It has too thick an atmosphere.
    \item It is too large.
    \item It does not have a metallic core.
\end{enumerate}
\item Which living organisms most resemble the common ancestor of all life according to genetic testing?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item viruses
    \item bacteria such as E. coli
    \item organisms living deep in the oceans around seafloor volcanic vents and in hot springs
    \item plankton that use sunlight as an energy source through photosynthesis
\end{enumerate}
\item Which is the least likely cause of death?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item Being hit in the head by a bullet.
    \item Being hit by a small meteorite.
    \item Starvation during global winter caused by a major impact.
    \item Driving while intoxicated without wearing seatbelts.
\end{enumerate}
\end{enumerate}
