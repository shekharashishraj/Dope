\section*{Multiple Choice (10 marks)}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\arabic*.]
\item In modern societies, social status is typically measured by a person's:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item age
    \item income
    \item verbal fluency
    \item occupation
\end{enumerate}
\item Booth's (1901) study revealed that the percentage of people in London who were living in poverty was:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item 30.70\%
    \item 27.50\%
    \item 18.60\%
    \item 33.20\%
\end{enumerate}
\item The 'third age' of the life course is said to involve:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item active non-work and independence after retirement
    \item full time employment, family-building and adult responsibility
    \item illness, isolation and increasing dependence on others
    \item the transition from education to work, and distinctive youth cultures
\end{enumerate}
\item Sreberny-Mohammadi (1996) argues that national cultures can resist American cultural domination of the media by:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item domesticating its content, including more 'home-produced' programmes
    \item controlling the distribution of imported products by banning satellite dishes
    \item creating 'reverse flows' of their own programmes back to imperial societies
    \item all of the above
\end{enumerate}
\item Ethnic identity refers to:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item the objective categories of ethnicity used in the census
    \item a felt sense of group membership on the basis of religion, language, or history
    \item the imposition of a racial label on a minority by a powerful majority
    \item the fragmented and pluralistic nature of ethnicity
\end{enumerate}
\end{enumerate}
