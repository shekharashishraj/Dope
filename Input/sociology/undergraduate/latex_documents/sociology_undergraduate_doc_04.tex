\section*{Multiple Choice (10 marks)}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\arabic*.]
\item An 'open' society is one that:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item grants every member equal status
    \item does not have any official secrets in its government
    \item has permissive attitudes towards sexual behaviour
    \item allows people to move between levels of the hierarchy
\end{enumerate}
\item In modern societies, social status is typically measured by a person's:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item age
    \item income
    \item verbal fluency
    \item occupation
\end{enumerate}
\item The term 'collective consumption' (Castells 1977) refers to:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item the privatization of public services by the Conservative government
    \item the lifestyle practice of shopping in peer groups
    \item the form of tuberculosis suffered by those who collect stamps
    \item the provision of health, housing, and education services by the state
\end{enumerate}
\item Which of these trends did the New Right not suggest as evidence of declining family values?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item the tendency for cohabitation before marriage
    \item the rising divorce rate
    \item the absence of fathers in many households
    \item the increasing number of single parent families
\end{enumerate}
\item An ecclesia is:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item a religious organization that claims total spiritual authority over its members
    \item a church organized around voluntary rather than compulsory membership
    \item a sect or cult with a very small following
    \item a hierarchy of priests or other spiritual leaders
\end{enumerate}
\end{enumerate}
