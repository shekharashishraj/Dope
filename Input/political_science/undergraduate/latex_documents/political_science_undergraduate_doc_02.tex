\section*{Multiple Choice (10 marks)}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\arabic*.]
\item How many states in the international system are likely to have nuclear weapons right now?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item Fewer than 7
    \item Between 8 and 15
    \item Between 16 and 25
    \item More than 25
\end{enumerate}
\item A member of the president's cabinet is said to have "gone native" when that cabinet member
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item resigns to take a position as a consultant to lobbying groups
    \item cedes control of his or her department to lifelong bureaucrats within the department
    \item places his or her department's priorities above the president's
    \item accepts bribes or expensive favors from businesses regulated by his or her department
\end{enumerate}
\item Which of the following presidential powers is shared with the Senate?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item Deploying troops
    \item Drafting appropriations bills
    \item Negotiating treaties
    \item Forcing Congress into session
\end{enumerate}
\item Which of the following most accurately describes the right of American citizens to privacy?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item The right to privacy is determined entirely by the states on a case-by-case basis.
    \item The right to privacy is explicitly granted in the Preamble to the Constitution.
    \item The Supreme Court has ruled that the right to privacy is implied by the Bill of Rights.
    \item Common law requires the government to respect citizens' right to privacy.
\end{enumerate}
\item Which of the following statements is the odd one out?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item Societal groups have a right to survive.
    \item Societal groups have their own reality.
    \item Societal groups are constituted by social interaction.
    \item Societal groups are multiple-identity units.
\end{enumerate}
\end{enumerate}
