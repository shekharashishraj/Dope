\section*{Multiple Choice (10 marks)}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\arabic*.]
\item What are the earnings per share of a company showing a profit to common shareholders of
\$4,750, if the company has 1,000 shares of stock outstanding.
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item \$4.25
    \item \$4.50
    \item \$5.25
    \item \$4.75
    \item \$6.50
\end{enumerate}
\item Many retail brands adopt a single umbrella brand, based on the name of the organization.
This is referred to as:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item Corporate brands.
    \item Generic branding.
    \item Manufacturer brand.
    \item Family branding.
    \item Product line branding.
\end{enumerate}
\item To evaluate an organization's Strategic Business Units (SBU), the Boston Consulting Group
developed a portfolio performance framework that characterizes some of the SBUs as:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item Cash outs.
    \item Silver bullets.
    \item Question marks.
    \item Risky ventures.
    \item Charging bulls.
\end{enumerate}
\item A noninterest-bearing note with a face value of \$600 and a term of 30 days dated April 5
was discounted April 15 at a rate of 5\%. What were the proceeds?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item \$597.50
    \item \$599.33
    \item \$596.67
    \item \$595
    \item \$604.17
\end{enumerate}
\item Express (2 / 5) and [1 / (100)] as a percentage.
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item 40\%, 0.25\%
    \item 40\%, 1\%
    \item 35\%, 0.75\%
    \item 50\%, 1\%
    \item 40\%, 0.5\%
\end{enumerate}
\end{enumerate}
