\item \textbf{Answer:} B
\\ \textit{Options:} A) Relevant documents are evenly distributed across rankings; B) The first relevant document appears early, but others are ranked poorly; C) All relevant documents appear at the top; D) No relevant documents are retrieved
\\ \textit{Note:} Correct option: B - MRR rewards finding one relevant item high early; MAP averages precision over all relevant items, so if later relevant docs rank badly, MAP can stay low while MRR stays high.
\item \textbf{Answer:} A
\\ \textit{Options:} A) It accounts for ranking positions of all relevant documents; B) It only considers the first relevant document; C) It ignores irrelevant documents; D) It requires fewer computations
\\ \textit{Note:} Correct option: A - MAP incorporates where every relevant document appears in the ranked list, not just a single cutoff like Precision@$k$.
\item \textbf{Answer:} B

\item \textbf{Answer:} A
\\ \textit{Options:} A) DPR requires labeled training data and may not generalize well; B) BM25 uses neural embeddings; C) BM25 ignores word frequencies; D) DPR does not use queries
