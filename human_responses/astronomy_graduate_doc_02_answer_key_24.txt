Long Form Response Questions

11. Sample answer:

Eratosthenes estimated the Earth's circumference by using the differences in the angle of the sun's rays at two locations, Alexandria and Syene, at the same time. 

Knowing the distance between the two locations and the angle of the sun at each location allowed him to calculate the Earth's orbit with remarkable accuracy for his time.


12. Sample answer:

The U.S.S.R.'s Mars lander missions, including the Mars 3 mission, were historically significant as they represented early efforts to explore Mars directly. 

Despite technical difficulties, these missions provided valuable data on Martian atmosphere, surface conditions, and potential for life, paving the way for future exploration and increasing our understanding of the planet.
