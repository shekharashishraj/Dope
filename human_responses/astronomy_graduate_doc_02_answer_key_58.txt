Long Form Response Questions

11. Sample answer:

Aristarchus used observations during a lunar eclipse to estimate the Earth’s size. 

He observed the shadow of the Earth on the Moon and compared the relative sizes of the Earth and Moon by calculating the time it took for the Moon to move through the Earth's shadow. 

He also used observations of the angle of the Sun at different latitudes and compared it to the angle of elevation at the same time in another location, which helped in estimating the circumference of the Earth.


12. Sample answer:

Although NASA’s rover missions have primarily focused on Mars, the exploration of Venus has been significant in terms of missions like Magellan, which mapped the Venusian surface with radar. 

These missions provided valuable data on Venus's geology, atmosphere, and volcanic activity, enhancing our understanding of its harsh surface conditions, atmospheric composition, and weather patterns. 

Venus's study helped compare terrestrial planet atmospheres and geological features, contributing vital insights into planetary evolution and solar system dynamics.
