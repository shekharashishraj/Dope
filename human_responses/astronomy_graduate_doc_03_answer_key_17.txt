Long Form Response Questions

11. Sample answer:

The Laniakea supercluster is a massive structure in the universe containing the Milky Way among other galaxies. 

Its discovery has reshaped our understanding of superclusters and has implications for cosmic flow fields affecting galaxies. 

Understanding the position within Laniakea helps astronomers map the gravitational influences that affect galaxy movements and provides insight into how cosmic structures interact on a massive scale.


12. Sample answer:

Pluto's long orbital period results in extreme seasonal variations, with each season lasting over 60 Earth years. 

This contributes to substantial changes in its thin atmosphere, which can freeze and fall to the surface as it moves further from the Sun. 

These long seasons also affect geological processes, potentially impacting surface features like its glaciers and nitrogen ice flows, revealing the complex dynamic processes in its environment.
