Long Form Response Questions

11. Sample answer:

The Virgo Cluster is one of the nearest galaxy clusters to the Milky Way and plays a crucial role in the study of cosmic structure. 

It helps astronomers understand the distribution and behavior of galaxies within the universe. 

Studying the Virgo Cluster allows insight into galaxy formation and evolution, interactions among galaxies, and the role of dark matter in clusters. 

Its gravitational influence offers a key perspective on the dynamics of the Local Group, including the Milky Way.


12. Sample answer:

Pluto's long orbital period results in extended seasons, each lasting over 60 Earth years, significantly affecting its climate and atmospheric dynamics. 

As it orbits the Sun, the variation in distance causes large temperature changes influencing sublimation and deposition cycles of ices on its surface, such as nitrogen, carbon monoxide, and methane. 

These variations can cause atmospheric pressure changes, affecting weather patterns. 

Understanding these processes provides insights into the complexities of planetary and atmospheric sciences, especially for distant irregular bodies.
