Long Form Response Questions

11. Sample answer:

A white dwarf is the remnant core of a star that has exhausted its nuclear fuel, typically composed of electron-degenerate matter. 

Formed when a low or medium-mass star ends its life cycle and sheds its outer layers, a white dwarf is very dense and no longer undergoes fusion reactions. 

Its significance lies in its radiant cooling, providing insights into stellar evolution, energy radiation, and thermal properties as it gradually dims over time.


12. Sample answer:

Mars's atmospheric pressure is low due to its thin atmosphere, which is partially a result of its weak magnetic field. 

Without a strong magnetic field, Mars is less able to shield itself from solar wind, contributing to atmospheric loss. 

The weak magnetic field and low atmospheric pressure influence Mars's geophysical characteristics by limiting the presence of liquid water on its surface and affecting erosion and weathering processes. 

This relationship is crucial for understanding the planet's climate history and potential for past life.
