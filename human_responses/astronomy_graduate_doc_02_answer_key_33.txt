Long Form Response Questions

11. Sample answer:

Eratosthenes estimated the Earth's size by using the difference in the angle of the sun's rays at noon between two locations, Alexandria and Syene (now Aswan), on the same day of the year. 

By noting that the sun was directly overhead at Syene and calculating the angle of the shadow in Alexandria, he could use simple geometry and the distance between the two cities to estimate the Earth's circumference. 

This was significant as it provided one of the earliest scientific measurements of the Earth's size, demonstrating the power of observation and reasoning.


12. Sample answer:

The USSR's Venera missions to Venus were historically significant as they were the first to successfully land on another planet and transmit data back to Earth, expanding our understanding of Venus’s atmosphere, surface conditions, and composition. 

Politically, these missions demonstrated Soviet prowess in space exploration during the Cold War era, marking significant achievements in the space race and influencing geopolitical dynamics by showcasing technological advancements. 

Scientific data from these missions have informed subsequent research and exploration of Venus.
