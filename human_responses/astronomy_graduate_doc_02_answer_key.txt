Long Form Response Questions

11. Sample answer:

The methodology employed by Eratosthenes involved observing the angles of shadows in different locations to estimate the Earth's circumference. 

He noticed that in Syene (modern-day Aswan, Egypt), the sun was directly overhead at noon during the summer solstice, casting no shadow, while in Alexandria, shadows were cast at an angle. 

By measuring this angle and knowing the distance between the two locations, he used geometry to estimate Earth's size.


12. Sample answer:

The USSR's lander missions to Venus, particularly the Venera program, were significant historically and politically. 

These missions provided the first detailed information about Venus's atmosphere and surface conditions, revealing a hot and hostile environment. 

Politically, the missions were part of the space race during the Cold War, showcasing Soviet space capabilities and contributing to our understanding of planetary science.
