11. Sample answer:

** Discuss the methodology employed by Eratosthenes to estimate the Earth's size in 240 B.C., including the significance of his observations in different latitudes.


- **Answer:** Eratosthenes estimated the Earth's size by measuring the angle of the Sun's rays at noon in Alexandria and comparing it to Syene, where the Sun was directly overhead at the same time. 

By measuring the shadow angle and knowing the distance between the two cities, he calculated the Earth's circumference. 

This method was significant because it relied on geometric principles and demonstrated the use of empirical observation and mathematical reasoning in understanding the Earth's size.

12. Sample answer:

** Discuss the historical significance and scientific contributions of the U.S.S.R.'s lander missions to Venus, analyzing their impact on our understanding of the planet.


- **Answer:** The U.S.S.R.'s Venera missions to Venus were pioneering endeavors that provided the first direct data about Venus's surface and atmosphere. 

These missions revealed the extremely high surface temperatures and pressures, the composition of the atmosphere, and provided images and data from the planet's surface. 

The findings significantly advanced our understanding of Venus, highlighting its harsh conditions and influencing future planetary exploration strategies.
