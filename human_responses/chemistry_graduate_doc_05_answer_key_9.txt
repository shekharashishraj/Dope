11. Sample answer:

To calculate: Half-life (\(t_{1/2}\)) = 5 minutes Formula: \(k = \frac{0.693}{t_{1/2}}\) \(k = \frac{0.693}{5}\) \(k \approx 0.1386 \, \text{min}^{-1}\)


12. Sample answer:

Volume at 20°C = 100 ml Use Charles's Law: \(V_1/T_1 = V_2/T_2\) Convert temperatures to Kelvin: 20°C = 293 K 10°C = 283 K \(V_2 = V_1 \times \frac{T_2}{T_1} = 100 \times \frac{283}{293}\) \(V_2 \approx 96.6 \, \text{ml}\) Charles’s Law shows that gas volume is directly proportional to temperature.
