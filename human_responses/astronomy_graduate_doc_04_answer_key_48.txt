Long Form Response Questions

11. Sample answer:

A black body is an idealized physical object that absorbs all incident electromagnetic radiation and re-radiates energy in a continuous spectrum. 

It is significant for understanding the radiation of stars, as stars are often modeled as black bodies to analyze their thermal properties and spectra.


12. Sample answer:

Venus’s rotation is very slow, taking about 243 Earth days to complete one rotation. 

This slow rotation affects atmospheric dynamics, leading to a very thick atmosphere with high pressure. 

The atmospheric pressure influences geophysical characteristics such as surface temperature and weather patterns.
