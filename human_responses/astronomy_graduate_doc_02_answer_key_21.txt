Long Form Response Questions

11. Sample answer:

** Discuss the methodology employed by Eratosthenes to estimate the Earth's size in 240 B.C., including the significance of his observations in different latitudes.


- **Answer:** Eratosthenes estimated the Earth's circumference by comparing the angles of the Sun's rays at two different locations in Egypt at noon during the summer solstice. 

In Syene (modern-day Aswan), the Sun was directly overhead, casting no shadow. In Alexandria, however, there was a shadow, indicating a different angle. 

By measuring the angle of the shadow in Alexandria and knowing the distance between the two cities, Eratosthenes used geometry to calculate the Earth's circumference with high accuracy. 

This experiment was significant as it demonstrated the spherical nature of the Earth and contributed significantly to geography and mathematics.

12. Sample answer:

** Discuss the historical significance and scientific contributions of the U.S.S.R.'s lander missions to Venus, analyzing their impact on our understanding of the planet.


- **Answer:** The U.S.S.R.'s Venera missions, specifically the landers, were pivotal in understanding Venus. 

They provided the first direct data from the surface, confirming high atmospheric pressures and temperatures, and revealed chemical compositions, including evidence of volcanic activity. 

The Venera missions debunked previous theories that Venus might have been Earth-like, showing an inhospitable environment. 

These missions laid the groundwork for future planetary exploration and increased our understanding of planetary atmospheres and geology in extreme conditions, having a lasting impact on planetary science.
