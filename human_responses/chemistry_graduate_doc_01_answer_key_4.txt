12. Sample answer:

The intensity ratios in the EPR spectrum are influenced by the hyperfine splitting caused by interactions with nearby nuclei. 

For the t-Bu radical, the intensity ratios reflect the equivalent environment of the methyl protons. 

Factors affecting these ratios include the g-factor, hyperfine coupling constants, and the electronic environment of the radical.
