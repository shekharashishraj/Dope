Long Form Response Questions

11. Sample answer:

The concept of a white dwarf in astronomy refers to the remains of a star that has exhausted its nuclear fuel and shed its outer layers. 

These stellar remnants are incredibly dense and hot, but they no longer undergo fusion reactions. 

White dwarfs serve as laboratories for understanding the radiation and thermal properties of stars as they transition to their final stages. 

They gradually cool and fade over time and represent the end state for stars like the Sun.


12. Sample answer:

Mars's atmospheric pressure and magnetic field strength are interrelated factors influencing the planet's geophysical characteristics. 

Mars has a weak magnetic field, which contributes to the lack of significant atmospheric protection from solar winds. 

This weak magnetic field has facilitated the gradual stripping away of the atmosphere, leading to its current thin state. 

As a result, Mars’s atmospheric pressure is much lower than Earth's, affecting the planet's climate, surface conditions, and potential to support life as we know it.
