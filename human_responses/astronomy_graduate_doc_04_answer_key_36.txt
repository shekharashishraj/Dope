Long Form Response Questions

11. Sample answer:

A white dwarf is a small, dense stellar remnant left after a star has exhausted its nuclear fuel and shed its outer layers, typically composed mostly of electron-degenerate matter. 

White dwarfs are significant in understanding stellar evolution as they represent the final evolutionary state of stars not massive enough to become neutron stars or black holes. 

The study of white dwarfs provides insights into the life cycle of stars, their radiation processes, and their thermal properties, as they gradually cool and fade over time, illustrating principles of degenerate matter and quantum mechanics.


12. Sample answer:

Mars's atmospheric pressure is relatively low, averaging around 6 millibars, which is a fraction of Earth's atmospheric pressure. 

This low pressure is largely due to Mars's weak magnetic field, which is insufficient to protect the planet from solar winds that strip away its atmosphere over time. 

Consequently, the thin atmosphere influences the planet's temperature, climate, and geophysical characteristics, making it incapable of supporting liquid water on the surface under current conditions. 

The lack of a significant magnetic field and thick atmosphere means Mars experiences high radiation levels and temperature variations, fundamentally affecting its potential to support life as we know it.
