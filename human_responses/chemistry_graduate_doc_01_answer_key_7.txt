11. Sample answer:

A typical E. coli chromosome consists of approximately 4.6 million base pairs. Assuming each base pair contributes about 0.34 nanometers to the total length of the DNA: Length = 4.6 × 10^6 base pairs × 0.34 nm/base pair = 1.564 mm Significance: This length is approximately 1,000 times longer than the cell itself, demonstrating the intricate and compact folding mechanism necessary to fit the DNA into the bacterial cell.


12. Sample answer:

The frequency shift in the EPR spectrum of the t-Bu radical is significant as it indicates hyperfine interactions between the unpaired electron and nearby atomic nuclei. 

Factors influencing this include: - Electron Spin Density: Distribution of the unpaired electron affects the hyperfine coupling. 

- Hyperfine Coupling Constants: Interaction strength with surrounding hydrogen nuclei. 

- Environmental Effects: Solvent and temperature can alter EPR signals. The analysis of these factors aids in understanding the structural and electronic characteristics of radical species, providing insight into reaction mechanisms.
