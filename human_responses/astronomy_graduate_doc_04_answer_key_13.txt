Long Form Response Questions

11. Sample answer:

A black body in astronomy is an idealized object that absorbs all incident electromagnetic radiation, regardless of frequency or angle of incidence. 

Stars approximate black bodies, enabling astronomers to use black body radiation to determine their temperature and other thermal properties. 

Understanding black bodies helps in studying the distribution and intensity of radiation emitted by stars and other celestial objects.


12. Sample answer:

Venus rotates very slowly, once every 243 Earth days, and in the opposite direction to its orbit. 

This slow rotation affects its magnetic field, which is much weaker compared to Earth's. 

The weak magnetic field allows solar winds to interact more directly with Venus's atmosphere, influencing its climatic conditions, such as the stripping of atmospheric gases and the creation of phenomena like the super-rotation of its clouds.
