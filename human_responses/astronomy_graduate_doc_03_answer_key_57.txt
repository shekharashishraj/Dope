Long Form Response Questions

11. Sample answer:

The Virgo Cluster is one of the nearest galaxy clusters to the Milky Way and plays a crucial role in understanding cosmic structure. 

It helps astronomers study the distribution of galaxies and the nature of dark matter due to its massive gravitational pull affecting nearby galaxies. 

This cluster offers insights into galaxy formation, the behavior of galaxy clusters, and the large-scale structure of the universe.


12. Sample answer:

Pluto's orbital period of approximately 250 years significantly impacts its geological and atmospheric characteristics. 

The long orbit results in extreme seasonal changes, affecting surface temperature and atmospheric composition over decades. 

The variations in sunlight and distance from the Sun lead to changes in Pluto’s ice and gas distribution, influencing its climate and surface features over these extensive seasons.
