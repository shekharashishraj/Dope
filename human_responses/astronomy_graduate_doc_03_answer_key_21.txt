Long Form Response Questions

11. Sample answer:

The Virgo Cluster is a massive cluster of galaxies located about 53 million light-years away in the constellation Virgo. 

It is a significant feature of our cosmic neighborhood and plays a crucial role in the Local Supercluster's gravitational dynamics. 

The Milky Way, as part of the Local Group of galaxies, is influenced by the Virgo Cluster through gravitational interactions that affect its motion and evolution. 

Understanding the Virgo Cluster helps astronomers learn about the large-scale structure of the universe, galaxy formation, and the distribution of dark matter. 

Its study provides insights into the hierarchical nature of cosmic structures, revealing how galaxies and clusters are distributed across the universe.


12. Sample answer:

Pluto’s lengthy orbital period of about 248 years results in extended seasons, with each lasting over 60 Earth years. 

This elongated orbit causes drastic changes in distance from the Sun, impacting its geological and atmospheric conditions. 

During its closest approach, or perihelion, Pluto experiences warming that could lead to sublimation of surface ices, creating a tenuous atmosphere. 

Conversely, during its aphelion, the atmosphere might freeze and fall back onto the surface due to extreme cooling. 

These seasonal changes influence surface geology, including potential shifting of ice masses and triggering cryovolcanism. 

Understanding these dynamics offers insights into the complex interaction between orbital mechanics and planetary environments.
