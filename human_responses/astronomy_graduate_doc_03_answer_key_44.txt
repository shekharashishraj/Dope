11. Sample answer:

The Laniakea Supercluster is significant because it encompasses the Milky Way and shows our galaxy's location in a larger cosmic structure. 

This helps astronomers understand the gravitational influences affecting our galaxy and enhances our knowledge of cosmic distribution and the interconnectedness of galaxies.


12. Sample answer:

Pluto's long orbital period of approximately 250 years affects its geological and atmospheric characteristics significantly. 

The lengthy orbit results in prolonged seasonal changes, meaning each season lasts for several decades. 

This influences the temperature, surface conditions, and atmospheric composition, contributing to dynamic changes over its orbit.
