Long Form Response Questions

11. Sample answer:

In astronomy, a black body is an idealized physical object that absorbs all incident electromagnetic radiation, regardless of frequency or angle of incidence. 

Since it reflects no light, the appearance of a perfect black body is dictated solely by its temperature. 

The concept is significant because it helps in understanding the radiation emitted by stars, based on their thermal properties through Planck's law, Wien's displacement law, and the Stefan-Boltzmann law, which relate the radiation's spectral distribution and total energy to temperature.


12. Sample answer:

Venus has a very slow rotational speed, taking about 243 Earth days to complete one rotation and rotating in the opposite direction of most planets in the solar system. 

This slow rotation might contribute to the planet's extreme surface temperature, along with its dense atmosphere rich in carbon dioxide, leading to a runaway greenhouse effect that traps heat. 

The lack of significant rotational cooling and strong atmospheric winds distribute heat evenly across the planet, resulting in uniform surface temperatures.
