Long Form Response Questions

11. Sample answer:

The Holy Mother of Mount Fairy Peach holds a significant place in Silla culture as a representation of fertility, protection, and prosperity. 

Her influence would often extend into religious practices that focus on agricultural abundance and communal harmony. 

Devotees may have offered prayers and rituals seeking her blessings for successful harvests and societal prosperity, indicating her pivotal role in both personal and communal life. 

Her influence likely reinforced societal values of reverence for nature, community welfare, and the importance of harmony between humans and the divine.


12. Sample answer:

Gurbani hymns in Sikh literature serve as the spiritual and theological foundation for Sikh beliefs. 

They articulate the key principles of the Sikh faith, such as the oneness of God, the importance of living truthfully, selfless service, and devotion. 

These hymns guide moral conduct and spiritual enlightenment. The narratives conveyed through gurbani offer insights into the life of the Gurus and the historical context of Sikhism’s development. 

They emphasize values like equality, social justice, and community service, and continue to be central in shaping the communal and spiritual identity of Sikhs worldwide.
