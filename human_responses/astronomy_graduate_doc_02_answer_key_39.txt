Long Form Response Questions

11. Sample answer:

** Discuss the methodology employed by Eratosthenes to estimate the Earth's size in 240 B.C., including the significance of his observations in different latitudes.


- **Answer:** Eratosthenes used observations of the Sun's angle at noon in two different locations: Syene and Alexandria. 

At noon on the summer solstice in Syene, the Sun was directly overhead, casting no shadow. 

Meanwhile, in Alexandria, there was a measurable angle of shadow. By knowing the distance between the two cities and using simple geometry, Eratosthenes calculated the Earth's circumference with remarkable accuracy.



**

12. Sample answer:

** Discuss the historical significance and scientific contributions of the U.S.S.R.'s lander missions to Venus, analyzing their impact on our understanding of the planet.


- **Answer:** The U.S.S.R.'s Venera missions to Venus were groundbreaking. They provided the first images from the surface of another planet and sent back data on Venus's atmosphere and surface conditions. 

These missions revealed Venus's high surface pressure and temperature, predominantly carbon dioxide atmosphere, and helped shape our understanding of greenhouses gases and planetary climates, contributing significantly to comparative planetology.
