11. Sample answer:

** Discuss the methodology employed by Eratosthenes to estimate the Earth's size in 240 B.C., including the significance of his observations in different latitudes.

    

**Answer:** Eratosthenes estimated the Earth's size by using the difference in the angle of the Sun's rays at two locations a known distance apart: Alexandria and Syene (now Aswan). 

At noon during the summer solstice, the Sun was directly overhead in Syene but not in Alexandria. 

By measuring the angle of shadow in Alexandria and knowing the distance between the two cities, he used geometry to calculate the Earth's circumference. 

This was significant as it provided one of the earliest known measurements of Earth's size using observations from different latitudes, demonstrating the spherical nature of the Earth.

12. Sample answer:

** Discuss the historical significance and scientific contributions of the U.S.S.R.'s lander missions to Venus, analyzing their impact on our understanding of the planet.

    

**Answer:** The U.S.S.R.'s Venera lander missions were significant as they provided the first direct data from Venus's surface, confirming extreme surface temperatures and pressures that were previously theorized. 

These missions also returned the first photographs from the surface of another planet, contributing greatly to our understanding of Venus's atmosphere and surface conditions. 

The information gathered helped to establish a clearer picture of Venus's composition, atmospheric chemistry, and possible volcanic activity, shaping subsequent theories about its geological development and climate. 

These missions marked a major milestone in planetary exploration and influenced future missions to inner planets.
