Long Form Response Questions

11. Sample answer:

A black body in astronomy is an idealized object that absorbs all incident electromagnetic radiation, regardless of frequency or angle of incidence. 

It emits radiation at a characteristic spectrum that depends only on its temperature, described by Planck's law of black-body radiation. 

The significance in astronomy is immense, as stars closely resemble black bodies. 

By analyzing their spectra, astronomers can determine a star's temperature, luminosity, and composition data critical for understanding stellar and galactic evolution.


12. Sample answer:

Venus has a very slow rotational speed, taking about 243 Earth days to complete a rotation. 

This slow rotation has minimal impact on its surface temperature due to the thick atmosphere dominated by CO2, which creates a strong greenhouse effect. 

This greenhouse effect results in extremely high surface temperatures that remain fairly uniform. 

The planet's geophysical characteristics, such as lack of significant temperature variation between day and night, are influenced more by the atmospheric composition and pressure rather than rotational speed.
