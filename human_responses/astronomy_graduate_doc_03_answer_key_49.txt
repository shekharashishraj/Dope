Long Form Response Questions

11. Sample answer:

The Virgo Cluster is a significant cluster of galaxies that is relatively close to the Milky Way. 

Understanding its structure and the gravitational interactions within it helps astronomers study the distribution of galaxies and the larger-scale structure of the universe. 

As a part of the Virgo Supercluster, the study of the Virgo Cluster provides insights into how galaxies are grouped, their motion, and the role of dark matter in galaxy formation and dynamics.


12. Sample answer:

Pluto's orbital period of approximately 250 years has significant implications for its geological and atmospheric characteristics. 

The lengthy orbit results in extreme seasonal changes, with each season lasting about 60 Earth years. 

This affects the temperature and atmospheric composition on Pluto, causing the atmosphere to freeze and collapse partially or entirely during its orbit. 

These long seasons influence surface features and the potential for atmospheric and geological activity, with significant implications for understanding the evolution of small icy bodies in the outer solar system.
