Long Form Response Questions

11. Sample answer:

A white dwarf is the remnant core of a star that has exhausted its nuclear fuel and shed its outer layers. 

It is composed mostly of electron-degenerate matter. White dwarfs are significant because they emit radiation primarily through residual thermal energy, and their study provides insights into stellar evolution and the fate of stars.


12. Sample answer:

Mars’s rotational speed is relatively similar to Earth’s, but it have a weak magnetic field because it lacks a geodynamo driven by a liquid metallic core like Earth. 

This affects atmospheric retention and surface exposure to solar and cosmic radiation, influencing its geophysical and possibly it's atmospheric characteristics.
