Long Form Response Questions

11. Sample answer:

** Discuss the methodology employed by Eratosthenes to estimate the Earth's size in 240 B.C., including the significance of his observations in different latitudes.



- **Answer:** Eratosthenes used the difference in the angle of the sun's rays at two different locations, Syene and Alexandria, at the same time of day to estimate the Earth's circumference. 

He knew that in Syene (modern-day Aswan), the sun was directly overhead at noon during the summer solstice, casting no shadow, whereas in Alexandria, it cast a shadow. 

By measuring the angle of the shadow and knowing the distance between the two cities, he was able to use geometry to estimate the Earth's circumference. 

This was significant because it was one of the first known scientific measurements of the Earth's size and demonstrated the application of geometry to solve real-world problems.

12. Sample answer:

** Discuss the historical significance and scientific contributions of the U.S.S.R.'s lander missions to Venus, analyzing their impact on our understanding of the planet.



- **Answer:** The U.S.S.R.'s Venera missions were pioneering efforts that provided the first direct data from the surface of Venus. 

These missions contributed significantly to our understanding of Venus's atmosphere, surface conditions, and geology. 

The Venera landers were the first human-made objects to enter another planet's atmosphere and transmit data back to Earth, including images and analyses of the dense, hot atmosphere. 

They helped confirm the presence of clouds of sulfuric acid and extreme surface temperatures and pressures, drastically improving our understanding of Venus and debunking earlier theories about its habitability. 

These missions also set the stage for future planetary exploration.
