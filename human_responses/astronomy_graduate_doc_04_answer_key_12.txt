Long Form Response Questions

11. Sample answer:

A black body in astronomy is an idealized physical body that absorbs all incident electromagnetic radiation, regardless of frequency or angle of incidence. 

It emits radiation in a predictable and continuous spectrum, determined solely by its temperature, known as black body radiation. 

The concept is significant in understanding stars since stars approximate black bodies, allowing astronomers to determine their temperature, composition, and age by analyzing the emitted spectrum.


12. Sample answer:

Venus has a very slow rotational speed and lacks a significant magnetic field, which influences its climatic conditions. 

The slow rotation means Venus does not have the kind of dynamo effect that generates Earth's magnetic field. 

As a result, solar winds directly interact with its atmosphere, contributing to atmospheric loss. 

These factors, coupled with a thick, CO2-rich atmosphere, create a runaway greenhouse effect, leading to high surface temperatures and extreme climatic conditions.
