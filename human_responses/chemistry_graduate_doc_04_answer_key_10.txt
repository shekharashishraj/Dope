11. Sample answer:

To calculate the final pressure after 20 g of Dry Ice (CO₂) sublimates in a sealed 0.75-liter bottle at 25°C, apply the ideal gas law: \( PV = nRT \). 

Mass of CO₂: 20 g Molar mass of CO₂: 44.01 g/mol Moles of CO₂ (n): \( \frac{20}{44.01} \approx 0.454 \) mol Volume (V): 0.75 L Temperature (T): 25°C = 298 K R (Ideal gas constant): 0.0821 L·atm/mol·K Calculate Pressure (P): \[ P = \frac{nRT}{V} = \frac{0.454 \times 0.0821 \times 298}{0.75} \approx 14.8 \text{ atm} \]


12. Sample answer:

To calculate the thickness of an oil film using the volume and area given: Volume of oil: 1 mm³ = \( 1 \times 10^{-3} \) cm³ Area covered: 1 m² = \( 10,000 \) cm² Thickness (T): \[ T = \frac{\text{Volume}}{\text{Area}} = \frac{1 \times 10^{-3}}{10,000} = 1 \times 10^{-7} \text{ cm} \] Convert to angstroms: \[ 1 \text{ cm} = 10^{8} \text{ angstroms} \] \[ 1 \times 10^{-7} \text{ cm} = 10 \text{ angstroms} \] The thickness of the oil film is 10 angstroms.
