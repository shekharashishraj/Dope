Long Form Response Questions

11. Sample answer:

The Laniakea supercluster is a massive cosmic structure that includes the Milky Way and approximately 100,000 other galaxies. 

It plays a crucial role in understanding the distribution of galaxies in the universe, the gravitational influences between galaxies, and the flow of cosmic material. 

This structure helps astronomers and cosmologists understand the connectivity and large-scale organization of the universe, providing insights into the gravitational forces that shape cosmic evolution. 

The concept also challenges the perception of isolated groups of galaxies by showing how interconnected they are within larger structures, expanding our understanding of our galaxy's place in the universe.


12. Sample answer:

Pluto's long orbital period of approximately 250 years results in extreme seasonal variations due to its elliptical orbit. 

As Pluto moves closer and then farther from the Sun, the solar energy it receives varies significantly. 

This affects temperature and atmospheric pressure, leading to changes in sublimation and deposition of surface ices, which might include nitrogen, methane, and carbon monoxide. 

These changes potentially cause variations in Pluto's thin atmosphere and surface features such as ice cap growth and erosion. 

The lengthy seasonal cycle presents a unique climate system and geological activity, providing a distinct laboratory for studying comparative planetology and climate dynamics in the outer solar system.
