Long Form Response Questions

11. Sample answer:

Eratosthenes estimated the Earth's size by measuring the angle of the Sun's rays in two different locations, Alexandria and Syene. 

On the summer solstice, when the Sun was directly overhead in Syene, he measured the shadow in Alexandria and found it formed an angle of about 7.2 degrees. 

Knowing the distance between the two cities, he used geometry to calculate the Earth's circumference. 

This methodology was significant because it demonstrated the concept of a spherical Earth and the ability to measure large-scale features using basic observations and mathematics.


12. Sample answer:

The U.S.S.R.'s lander missions to Venus, notably the Venera program, were crucial in advancing our understanding of Venus’s atmosphere, surface conditions, and environmental conditions. 

The missions provided the first images from the surface of another planet and revealed high surface temperatures and pressures, as well as a predominantly carbon dioxide atmosphere. 

Politically, these missions symbolized Soviet prowess in space exploration during the Cold War, challenging the U.S. 

and intensifying the space race. They advanced planetary science and underscored the importance of space exploration in geopolitical strategies.
