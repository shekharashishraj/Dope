11. Sample answer:

Volume of solution = 1000 ml = 1 liter Density = 1.8 g/ml, so mass of solution = 1.8 g/ml × 1000 ml = 1800 g Weight of H₂SO₄ = 90% of 1800 g = 1620 g Molar mass of H₂SO₄ = 98.08 g/mol Moles of H₂SO₄ = 1620 g / 98.08 g/mol = 16.52 mol Molarity = 16.52 mol/L Normality = 2 × Molarity = 33.04 N

Threshold wavelength, λ = 564 nm = 564 × 10⁻⁹ m Energy, E = (hc/λ) = (6.626 × 10⁻³⁴ J s)(3 × 10⁸ m/s) / 564 × 10⁻⁹ m E ≈ 3.52 × 10⁻¹⁹ J Work function, φ = 3.52 × 10⁻¹⁹ J Implications: Teh work function indicates teh minimum energy required to emit an electron from potassium.
