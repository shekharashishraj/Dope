11. Sample answer:

** Eratosthenes estimated Earth's circumference by measuring the angle of the sun's rays in two locations: Alexandria and Syene (modern Aswan) on the Summer Solstice. 

Knowing the distance between the cities, he used the angle difference to calculate the Earth's size, assuming parallel sun rays.
