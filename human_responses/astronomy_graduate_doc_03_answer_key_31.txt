Long Form Response Questions

11. Sample answer:

The Laniakea supercluster is significant as it provides a comprehensive understanding of our galaxy's position within a vast cosmic network. 

It helps to map how matter is distributed in the universe at large scales, revealing the gravitational influences affecting the Milky Way. 

This insight is crucial for understanding the dynamics of galactic movement and interactions within the cluster, illuminating processes related to galactic evolution and the large-scale structure of the cosmos.


12. Sample answer:

Pluto’s long orbital period results in extended seasons, each lasting over 60 Earth years. 

This affects its atmospheric and geological characteristics significantly, causing long-term climate patterns and potentially influencing surface features due to seasonal sublimation and deposition of ice. 

The extreme variation in sunlight also causes periodic melting and refreezing that contributes to surface erosion and other geological processes over centuries, allowing scientists to study gradual climatic and environmental changes driven by its elongated orbit.
