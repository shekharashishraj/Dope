Long Form Response Questions

11. Sample answer:

The Virgo Supercluster is a massive group of galaxies, including the Milky Way, that plays a critical role in our understanding of cosmic structure. 

It highlights the hierarchical nature of the universe, showcasing how galaxies are organized into clusters and superclusters, influencing their dynamics and evolution. 

The Milky Way's position within the Virgo Supercluster helps astronomers study galaxy formation, interaction, and distribution on large scales, therefore enhancing the comprehension of the universe's overall structure.


12. Sample answer:

Pluto's long orbital period of around 250 years means that its seasons are extremely prolonged compared to Earth’s. 

This has significant effects on its geological and atmospheric characteristics. The extended seasons can lead to substantial temperature variations, influencing ice and gas distribution on the planet’s surface and atmosphere, and affecting surface features. 

During its orbit, parts of Pluto may experience many years of continuous sunlight or darkness, impacting its climate and atmospheric dynamics, such as sublimation of nitrogen ice and atmospheric pressure changes.
