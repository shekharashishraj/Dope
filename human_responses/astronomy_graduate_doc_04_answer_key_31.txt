Long Form Response Questions

11. Sample answer:

A black body is an idealized physical object that absorbs all incident electromagnetic radiation, regardless of frequency or angle of incidence. 

In astronomy, black bodies are important because they serve as a model for understanding how stars emit radiation. 

The spectrum of a black body at a given temperature can be used to infer the temperature and thermal properties of stars. 

This understanding helps astronomers determine the energy output and life cycle of stars, as black bodies emit a characteristic spectrum known as a Planck curve, which is dependent solely on temperature.


12. Sample answer:

Venus has a very slow rotational speed, taking about 243 Earth days to complete one rotation. 

This slow rotation is thought to contribute to the planet’s weak magnetic field, which is much weaker than Earth's. 

A strong magnetic field is generally generated by a planet's dynamo effect, which requires a combination of rotation and convection in its liquid core. 

Due to Venus's slow rotation, the dynamo effect is greatly reduced, resulting in a weak magnetic field. 

This weak magnetic field has implications for its atmospheric composition, as it allows solar wind to interact more directly with the atmosphere, potentially causing atmospheric stripping and affecting the retention of gases over time.
