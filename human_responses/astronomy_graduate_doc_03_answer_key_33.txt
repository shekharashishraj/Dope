Long Form Response Questions

11. Sample answer:

The significance of the Laniakea supercluster lies in its role as a large-scale cosmic structure that helps us understand the Milky Way’s position within a broader cosmic context. 

This supercluster organizes galaxies, including ours, within a gravitational framework that influences our movement through space. 

Its study provides insight into the distribution of matter in the universe and helps refine models of cosmic evolution.


12. Sample answer:

Pluto’s lengthy orbital period of approximately 250 years leads to extended seasonal cycles and significant climatic variations. 

Due to its extreme axial tilt and elliptical orbit, regions on Pluto experience prolonged periods of sunlight or darkness, affecting surface temperature and atmospheric pressure. 

This results in volatile movement and changes, impacting geological and atmospheric dynamics.
