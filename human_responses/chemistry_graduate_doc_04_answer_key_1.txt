11. Sample answer:

To calculate the final pressure, use the ideal gas law: 

PV = nRT

First, calculate the number of moles of CO₂:

Molar mass of CO₂ = 44 g/mol
n = 20 g/44 g/mol = 0.4545 mol

Given R = 0.0821 L atm/mol K and T = 25°C = 298 K:

P = nRT/V
P = 0.4545 * 0.0821 * 298/0.75
P ≈ 15.0 atm

Underlying Principle: The behavior of an ideal gas is described by the ideal gas law, where the pressure, volume, and temperature are directly related to the number of moles of the gas.


12. Sample answer:

Convert the volume from cubic millimeters to cubic meters: 

1 mm³ = 1 x 10⁻⁹ m³

Given the area of 1 m²:

The thickness t is given by the volume divided by the area:

t = Volume/Area = 1 x 10⁻⁹ m³/1 m² = 1 x 10⁻⁹ m

Convert meters to angstroms:

1 m = 10¹⁰ Å
t = 1 x 10⁻⁹ m x 10¹⁰ Å/m = 10 Å

Underlying Principle: The thickness of a thin film is calculated by dividing the volume of the substance by the area it covers. 

Converting to angstroms provides a more practical unit for atomic scale distances.
