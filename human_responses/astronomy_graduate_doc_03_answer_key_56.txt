Long Form Response Questions

11. Sample answer:

The Laniakea supercluster is a massive cosmic structure that contains the Milky Way galaxy among hundreds of thousands of other galaxies. 

Its discovery helps astronomers understand the large-scale structure of the universe, showcasing how galaxies are interconnected within a vast network. 

This knowledge challenges and enhances our understanding of cosmic flow, the expansion of the universe, and the gravitational influences that shape galaxy clusters.


12. Sample answer:

Pluto's long orbital period means that its seasons are extremely lengthy, with each lasting over 60 Earth years. 

This extended time frame leads to severe and prolonged climatic conditions, significantly affecting its surface and atmospheric dynamics. 

As it moves further from the Sun, temperatures drop, causing its thin atmosphere to freeze onto its surface. 

Conversely, as it approaches the Sun, sublimation occurs, thickening the atmosphere. 

Such drastic cyclic changes influence not only the atmospheric pressure variations but also potential geological activity from sublimation processes.
