Long Form Response Questions

11. Sample answer:

Aristarchus estimated the Earth's size using observations of lunar eclipses and the angle of the Sun at different latitudes. 

By measuring the angle of the Sun at noon in two different locations, one directly north of the other, he could use the difference in angle and the known distance between the locations to calculate the Earth's circumference. 

This early form of trigonometry allowed him to derive surprisingly accurate results for his time.


12. Sample answer:

NASA's rover missions to Venus have been limited, with most exploration conducted via orbiters and probes. 

However, the data collected by various missions have significantly advanced our understanding of Venus's atmosphere, surface conditions, and potential volcanic activity. 

The missions have revealed extreme temperatures, high-pressure atmospheres rich in carbon dioxide, and clouds of sulfuric acid, reshaping our view of Venus from a potentially habitable twin of Earth to a hostile environment. 

These findings underscore the need to understand atmospheric processes that contribute to such harsh conditions and have implications for studying climate change on Earth.
