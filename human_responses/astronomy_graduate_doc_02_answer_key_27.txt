Long Form Response Questions

11. Sample answer:

Eratosthenes used observations of the angle of the sun's rays at noon in two different locations: Alexandria and Syene (now Aswan). 

At noon on the summer solstice in Syene, the sun was directly overhead, as evidenced by no shadow cast by a stick. 

Meanwhile, in Alexandria, shadows were cast, indicating an angular difference due to Earth's curvature. 

By measuring the angle of the shadow in Alexandria and knowing the distance between the two cities, Eratosthenes calculated Earth's circumference. 

This was significant as it provided one of the first known measurements of Earth's size, demonstrating Earth's roundness.


12. Sample answer:

The U.S.S.R.’s Venera program, which sent landers to Venus, was pivotal both scientifically and politically. 

Scientifically, the missions provided unprecedented data on Venus's atmosphere, surface conditions, and environmental phenomena, such as high temperatures and pressures, and acidic clouds. 

Politically, the missions demonstrated Soviet space technology advances during the Cold War, significantly contributing to the space race narrative. 

These missions showed the capability to land and operate probes under extreme conditions, enhancing global understanding of planetary science and the challenges of space exploration.
