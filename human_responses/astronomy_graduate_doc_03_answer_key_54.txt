Long Form Response Questions

11. Sample answer:

The Virgo Cluster is a significant collection of galaxies situated approximately 53 million light-years away from Earth. 

It is the nearest large galaxy cluster to the Milky Way and contains about 1,300 to 2,000 galaxies. 

Its significance lies in providing a detailed laboratory for studying galaxy interactions, dynamics, and evolution. 

The cluster's gravitational effects are thought to influence the local group of galaxies, including the Milky Way. 

Understanding the Virgo Cluster helps astronomers map the Universe's large-scale structure and explore dark matter distribution and cosmic flows within and beyond the Local Supercluster.


12. Sample answer:

Pluto's long orbital period results in extended seasons, with each lasting over 60 years. 

Its elliptical orbit causes significant variations in its distance from the Sun, impacting temperature and atmospheric pressure. 

During closer approaches, the atmosphere expands due to sublimation of surface ice, while moving away leads to atmospheric collapse as gases freeze. 

These extreme changes influence surface geology, as active processes like sublimation and deposition reshape Pluto's surface. 

Understanding Pluto's orbit allows scientists to explore its dynamic climate and contribute insights into other distant celestial bodies' behavior and evolution.
