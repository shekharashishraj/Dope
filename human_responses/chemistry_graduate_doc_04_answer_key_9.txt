11. Sample answer:

To calculate the final pressure, apply the ideal gas law: \( PV = nRT \). - Moles of CO₂: \( \frac{20 g}{44 g/mol} \approx 0.455 \) mol - Using \( R = 0.0821 \, \text{L atm/mol K} \), \( T = 25°C = 298 K \). 

- \( P = \frac{nRT}{V} = \frac{0.455 \times 0.0821 \times 298}{1} \approx 11.1 \, \text{atm} \).


12. Sample answer:

- Volume of oil = \( 1 \text{ mm}^3 = 1 \times 10^{-6} \text{ m}^3 \). - Area = \( 1 \text{ m}^2 \). 

- Thickness = \(\frac{\text{Volume}}{\text{Area}} = \frac{1 \times 10^{-6} \text{ m}^3}{1 \text{ m}^2} = 1 \times 10^{-6} \text{ m}\). 

- Convert to angstroms: \( 1 \text{ m} = 10^{10} \text{ angstroms} \). - Thickness in angstroms: \( 1 \times 10^{-6} \times 10^{10} = 10,000 \text{ angstroms} \).
