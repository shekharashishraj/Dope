11. Sample answer:

The Laniakea supercluster is a vast cosmic structure that includes the Milky Way galaxy. 

It represents a significant portion of the observable universe, showcasing the complex web-like structure of galaxies and galaxy clusters. 

Understanding Laniakea provides insights into the large-scale structure of the universe, the gravitational interactions between galaxies, and the distribution of dark matter. 

It highlights the interconnectedness of galaxies and aids in understanding the dynamics and evolution of cosmic structures.


12. Sample answer:

Pluto’s long orbital period results in extended seasons, with each lasting for several decades. 

This affects its geological and atmospheric characteristics, as the prolonged exposure to the Sun during certain periods can lead to sublimation of surface ices, altering Pluto's surface and atmospheric composition. 

The lengthy orbit also means that the climate is subject to substantial changes over time, impacting surface temperature and pressure, and possibly driving geological activity as the atmosphere freezes and thaws.
