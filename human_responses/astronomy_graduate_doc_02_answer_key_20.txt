11. Sample answer:

**
Discuss the methodology employed by Eratosthenes to estimate the Earth's size in 240 B.C., including the significance of his observations in different latitudes.


- **Answer:** Eratosthenes used the difference in the angle of the sun's rays at two different locations: Alexandria and Syene (now Aswan). 

In Syene, the sun was directly overhead at noon during the summer solstice, while in Alexandria, it cast a shadow, creating an angle. 

By measuring the angle and knowing the distance between the two cities, Eratosthenes calculated the Earth's circumference. 

This methodology was significant because it used geometric principles and provided a relatively accurate measurement for the Earth's size for the first time.

12. Sample answer:

**
Discuss the historical significance and scientific contributions of the U.S.S.R.'s lander missions to Venus, analyzing their impact on our understanding of the planet.


- **Answer:** The U.S.S.R.’s lander missions to Venus, particularly the Venera program, were historically significant because they were the first successful missions to land on another planet and send back data. 

They provided direct measurements of Venus's surface temperature and pressure, revealing it as an extremely hot and high-pressure environment. 

These missions improved our understanding of Venus's atmospheric composition and surface conditions, contributing to planetary science and demonstrating the capability of space exploration technology.
