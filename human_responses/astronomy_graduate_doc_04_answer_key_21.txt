Long Form Response Questions

11. Sample answer:

A black body in astronomy refers to an idealized object that absorbs all electromagnetic radiation falling onto it, without reflecting any light. 

It emits radiation at all wavelengths, and the spectrum of this emitted radiation is only dependent on the black body’s temperature. 

This concept is critical in understanding stars, as their radiation can be approximated by black body radiation, allowing scientists to determine a star's temperature, luminosity, and size. 

The Black Body Radiation Law, Planck’s Law, and the Stefan-Boltzmann Law, all help in understanding these fundamental properties of stars and their behaviors.


12. Sample answer:

Venus’s rotational speed is extremely slow, taking about 243 Earth days to complete one rotation. 

This slow rotation influences its magnetic field strength, which is very weak compared to that of Earth. 

The dynamo effect responsible for generating magnetic fields is less effective on Venus due to the slow rotation. 

This weak magnetic field results in less protection from solar wind and cosmic radiation, significantly affecting its atmosphere. 

The absence of a strong magnetic field and the planet’s thick atmosphere leads to substantial atmospheric superrotation, where the atmosphere circulates the planet much faster than the planet itself rotates, influencing its weather patterns and climate.
