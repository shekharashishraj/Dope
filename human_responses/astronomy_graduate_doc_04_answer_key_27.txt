Long Form Response Questions

11. Sample answer:

A white dwarf is the remnant core of a star that has exhausted its nuclear fuel and shed its outer layers. 

It is incredibly dense and consists mostly of electron-degenerate matter. Understanding white dwarfs is crucial for studying stellar evolution, thermal cooling, and the final stages of stellar life cycles.


12. Sample answer:

Mars’s thin atmosphere results from its weak magnetic field, which does not protect against solar winds that strip away atmospheric particles. 

This low atmospheric pressure affects surface conditions and potential habitability, influencing aspects like temperature fluctuations and erosion processes.
