Long Form Response Questions

11. Sample answer:

Eratosthenes estimated the Earth’s size by comparing the shadow of a stick in Syene (now Aswan) and in Alexandria at noon on the summer solstice. 

This allowed him to calculate the circumference of the Earth using the angle of the shadow and the distance between the two cities.


12. Sample answer:

The U.S.S.R.'s lander missions to Venus were historically significant as they provided the first direct measurements and photographs of the Venusian surface. 

These missions advanced scientific understanding and showcased Soviet technological capabilities during the space race, highlighting political competition with the United States.
