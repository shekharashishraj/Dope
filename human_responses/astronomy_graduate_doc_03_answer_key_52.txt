Laniakea Supercluster: The Laniakea Supercluster is significant because it encompasses the Milky Way and helps astronomers understand the structure and scale of the universe. 

It highlights the interconnectedness of galaxies within large cosmic filaments and nodes, impacting theories about galaxy formation and movement.

Pluto’s Orbital Period: Pluto’s long orbital period means that each season lasts for decades, significantly affecting its climate and atmospheric conditions. 

This slow orbit influences temperature variations across its surface, potentially impacting geophysical and atmospheric phenomena over prolonged timescales.
