11. Sample answer:

The answer given was "orbital mechanics," but generally, a black body is an idealized physical object that absorbs all incident electromagnetic radiation, regardless of frequency or angle of incidence. 

Black bodies are important in understanding the radiation of stars and their thermal properties because they provide a benchmark against which the thermal emission of real objects can be compared.
