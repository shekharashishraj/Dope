Long Form Response Questions

11. Sample answer:

A black body in astronomy is an idealized object that absorbs all incident electromagnetic radiation, regardless of frequency or angle of incidence. 

It emits radiation at a maximum rate for its given temperature. The concept is significant in understanding the radiation of stars and their thermal properties because it helps astronomers determine the temperature of stars by analyzing the spectrum of light they emit. 

By comparing real stellar spectra to that of an ideal black body, astronomers can deduce various properties, including surface temperature and luminosity.


12. Sample answer:

The slow rotational speed of Venus (one rotation every 243 Earth days) contributes to its extreme surface temperature, which averages around 465 degrees Celsius. 

This slow rotation affects atmospheric circulation, contributing to the planet's overall heat distribution. 

The dense atmosphere, composed mostly of carbon dioxide, creates a strong greenhouse effect, trapping heat and maintaining high surface temperatures. 

The slow rotation also minimizes temperature differences between day and night, with each side experiencing prolonged exposure to sunlight or darkness, influencing weather patterns and geological processes on the planet.
