Long Form Response Questions

11. Sample answer:

The Virgo Cluster is the nearest large galaxy cluster to our Milky Way and plays a crucial role in understanding the large-scale structure of the universe. 

It's part of the Virgo Supercluster, which also includes our Local Group of galaxies. 

Studying the Virgo Cluster helps astronomers understand galaxy formation and evolution, interactions between galaxies, and the distribution of dark matter. 

The cluster's influence affects the movement and future trajectory of the Milky Way within the cosmic web. 

Insights from the Virgo Cluster provide a laboratory for testing cosmological models and theories about the universe's expansion and structure.


12. Sample answer:

Pluto's orbital period of about 250 years leads to extremely long seasons, each lasting over 60 Earth years. 

This prolonged period results in significant variations in temperature and atmospheric composition as Pluto gets closer to, or farther from, the Sun. 

During its closest approach to the Sun (perihelion), Pluto's atmosphere becomes thicker due to sublimation of surface ices. 

As it moves away to aphelion, the atmosphere collapses, and frost forms on the surface. 

These conditions create a dynamic environment, influencing geological features like ice flows and erosion. 

Such long-term changes challenge our understanding of atmospheric physics and highlight the complexity of celestial bodies far from the Sun.
