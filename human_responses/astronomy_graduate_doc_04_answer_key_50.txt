Long Form Response Questions

11. Sample answer:

A white dwarf is the remnant core of a star that has exhausted the nuclear fuel in its core. 

It is significant in understanding the life cycle of stars, particularly those with masses similar to the Sun. 

White dwarfs are very dense and have a high temperature initially, but do not have an internal heat source, so they gradually cool and fade over time. 

They provide insights into stellar evolution and the thermal properties of dead stars.


12. Sample answer:

Mars's rotational speed is one of the factors that influence its magnetic field strength, although the planet's weak magnetic field is primarily due to its core dynamics. 

Mars rotates relatively quickly, about once every 24.6 hours, but it lacks a significant global magnetic field like Earth's because its core may have solidified. 

The weak magnetic field affects Mars's atmosphere and surface radiation levels, contributing to its geophysical characteristics.
