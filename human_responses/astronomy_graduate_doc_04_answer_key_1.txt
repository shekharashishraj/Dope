Long Form Response Questions

11. Sample answer:

A black body in astronomy refers to an idealized object that absorbs all incident electromagnetic radiation, regardless of frequency or angle of incidence. 

It also re-emits radiation in a characteristic spectrum that solely depends on its temperature, known as black body radiation. 

This concept is significant in astronomy because it provides a model for understanding the thermal radiation emitted by stars. 

By studying stellar spectra and comparing them to black body spectra, astronomers can determine the temperature, size, and composition of stars. 

The Stefan-Boltzmann law and Wien’s displacement law are critical in linking the temperature of a black body to its luminosity and peak emission wavelength, respectively, helping to infer the properties and mechanisms of stars.


12. Sample answer:

Venus has an extremely slow rotational speed, taking about 243 Earth days to complete one rotation. 

This slow rotation is a significant factor in Venus’s weak magnetic field, which is over 10,000 times weaker than Earth's. 

A planet's magnetic field is typically generated by the motion of conductive materials in its interior, a process driven by rotation. 

The lack of a substantial magnetic field allows Venus to have direct interactions with solar wind, which impacts its atmospheric composition. 

Due to this, the solar wind can strip away lighter atmospheric components like hydrogen, leaving a dense atmosphere dominated by heavy molecules such as carbon dioxide and nitrogen. 

The weak magnetic protection and slow rotation contribute to the build-up of a thick cloud cover and the extreme greenhouse effect that maintains Venus's high surface temperatures.
