Long Form Response Questions

11. Sample answer:

In Zen Buddhism, "honji" refers to the "original ground" or "original nature" that is the fundamental nature of reality. 

It emphasizes enlightenment and the realization that everything is interconnected. 

This concept is crucial as it highlights the importance of recognizing one's true nature beyond illusions and transient phenomena. 

It encourages practitioners to seek wisdom by understanding their inherent Buddha-nature, promoting detachment from illusions and fostering clarity.


12. Sample answer:

The Daoist concept of yin-yang represents the duality and harmony of opposite forces. 

Yin and yang are complementary, interconnected forces that illustrate the balance in the universe. 

In Daoist philosophy, this balance is essential to understanding the flow of life and achieving harmony with the Tao. 

The concept relates to "wu wei" or non-action, suggesting that one should align with the natural order and allow things to unfold naturally, without forceful intervention. 

This approach to life promotes peace, balance, and minimal interference with natural processes.
