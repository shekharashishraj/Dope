16. Sample answer:

Here's a sample function in Python:


def find_greater_than_m_n(numbers, m, n):
    return list(filter(lambda x: x > m and x > n, numbers))

# Example usage:
numbers_list = [10, 15, 20, 25, 30]
result = find_greater_than_m_n(numbers_list, 12, 18)
print(result)  # Output will be [20, 25, 30]
