Long Form Response Questions

11. Sample answer:

Laniakea, meaning "immense heaven" in Hawaiian, is the supercluster that includes the Milky Way. 

Its significance lies in redefining the boundaries of galactic superclusters using a novel approach based on the gravitational influence and the flow of galaxies. 

This cluster covers 100,000 galaxies stretched over 520 million light-years and has a gravitational center known as the Great Attractor. 

Understanding our position within Laniakea helps clarify the large-scale structure of the universe, providing insights into dark matter distribution, galaxy formation, and cosmic flow patterns. 

The discovery emphasizes the interconnectedness of galaxies and supports the idea of a web-like cosmos.


12. Sample answer:

Pluto’s long orbital period results in prolonged seasons, each lasting over 60 Earth years. 

This influences its surface temperatures and atmospheric conditions greatly, leading to complex weather patterns and potential geologic activity driven by seasonal temperature changes. 

As Pluto moves closer to the Sun, its atmosphere expands and becomes denser due to sublimating nitrogen ice, while moving away causes the atmosphere to freeze and fall back to the surface. 

This cycle impacts its geology by potentially triggering sublimation erosion and deposition processes, highlighting active surface-altering mechanisms despite its distance from the Sun.
