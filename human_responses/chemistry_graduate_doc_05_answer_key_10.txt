11. Sample answer:

For a first-order reaction, the relation is \( t_{1/2} = \frac{0.693}{k} \).
      

- \( k = \frac{0.693}{34 \text{ min}} \approx 0.0204 \text{ min}^{-1} \).
      - The rate constant \( k \) is inversely proportional to the half-life; as half-life decreases, \( k \) increases.


12. Sample answer:

According to Charles's Law, \( V_1/T_1 = V_2/T_2 \) (in Kelvin).
      - Convert temperatures to Kelvin: 20°C = 293 K, 10°C = 283 K.
      

- \( V_1 = 100 \text{ ml} \), \( V_2 = \frac{V_1 \cdot T_2}{T_1} = \frac{100 \cdot 283}{293} \approx 96.6 \text{ ml} \).
      

- Gas volume decreases with decreasing temperature when pressure and quantity of gas are constant.
