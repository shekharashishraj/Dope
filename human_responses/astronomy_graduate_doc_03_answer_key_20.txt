Long Form Response Questions

11. Sample answer:

The Laniakea Supercluster is a massive cosmic structure that includes the Milky Way as part of its vast expanse. 

Its discovery has been pivotal in enhancing our understanding of the universe’s large-scale structure. 

It suggests that galaxies are not evenly distributed but are part of interconnected structures influenced by gravitational attraction. 

The Milky Way's location within Laniakea helps astronomers understand our galaxy's position and movement within the cosmic web, providing insights into the forces shaping the universe's evolution.


12. Sample answer:

Pluto’s long orbital period means that it experiences extended seasons, each lasting for over two decades. 

This significantly impacts its surface and atmospheric conditions. The tilt of Pluto’s axis results in extreme variations in sunlight, affecting the temperature and causing the atmosphere to freeze and thicken as it moves away from the Sun. 

These changes can lead to sublimation and deposition processes that alter its surface features over time. 

As a result, Pluto’s climate and geological activity are profoundly influenced by its orbit, providing a unique context for studying planetary processes.
