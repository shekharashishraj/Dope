**Answer: True**

### Question 10
A comet's tail always points away from the Sun due to the solar wind and radiation pressure acting on the comet's gas and dust.

**Answer: True**

### Question 11
Discuss the methodology employed by Eratosthenes to estimate the Earth's size in 240 B.C., including the significance of his observations in different latitudes.

**Answer:**
Eratosthenes used a method involving the measurement of shadows. He knew that at noon during the summer solstice in Syene (modern-day Aswan, Egypt), the Sun was directly overhead, casting no shadow. Meanwhile, in Alexandria, a stick did cast a shadow, indicating the Sun was at an angle. By measuring the angle of the shadow in Alexandria and knowing the distance between the two cities, he calculated the Earth's circumference using simple geometry, demonstrating the significance of angular measurements at different latitudes.

### Question 12
Discuss the historical significance and scientific contributions of the U.S.S.R.'s lander missions to Venus, analyzing their impact on our understanding of the planet.

**Answer:**
The U.S.S.R.'s Venera missions to Venus were groundbreaking, as they were the first to land on another planet and transmit data back to Earth. They provided the first images from the surface of Venus and measured its atmospheric conditions, revealing a hot and high-pressure environment with thick clouds of sulfuric acid. These missions significantly advanced our understanding of Venusian geology and atmospheric science, highlighting the harsh conditions on Venus and influencing future planetary exploration missions.
