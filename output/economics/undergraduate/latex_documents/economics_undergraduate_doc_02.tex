\section*{Multiple Choice (10 marks)}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\arabic*.]
\item What are the effects an increase in the money supply on output on prices in the short run
and in the long run?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item Short run: increase in output, Long run: increase in general price level
    \item Short run: decrease in output, Long run: no change in general price level
    \item Short run: decrease in output, Long run: increase in general price level
    \item Short run: increase in general price level, Long run: decrease in output
    \item Short run: no change in output, Long run: increase in general price level
\end{enumerate}
\item Marginal cost always intersects average variable cost at
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item the minimum of average total cost
    \item the point where average variable cost is equal to marginal cost
    \item the profit-maximizing quantity
    \item the maximum of average variable cost
    \item the point of equilibrium between supply and demand
\end{enumerate}
\item Which of the following is an example of capital as an economic resource?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item A barrel of crude oil
    \item A music concert ticket
    \item A valuable painting
    \item An apartment for rent
    \item A bushel of wheat
\end{enumerate}
\item The price elasticity of demand for a product is greater if
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item the proportion of the good of the consumer's budget is high.
    \item the number of substitute products is limited.
    \item the product is unique and has no substitutes.
    \item the consumer has a high income.
    \item the period of time to respond to a price change is short.
\end{enumerate}
\item Investment demand most likely increases when
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item the stock market experiences a downturn.
    \item inflation rates rise.
    \item the real rate of interest rises.
    \item the economy enters a recession.
    \item global economic instability is high.
\end{enumerate}
\end{enumerate}
