\section*{Multiple Choice (10 marks)}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\arabic*.]
\item If a monopsony labor market suddenly were transformed into a perfectly competitive labor
market, how would the wage and employment change?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item The wage would decrease, but employment would remain constant.
    \item Both would increase.
    \item Both would decrease.
    \item The wage would remain constant, but employment would increase.
    \item The wage would increase, but employment would remain constant.
\end{enumerate}
\item A bilateral monopoly exists when
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item a monopsony competes with a monopoly
    \item a monopsony buys from a monopoly
    \item a monopolist sells two different types of goods
    \item two monopolies sell to a single consumer
    \item a monopoly competes with a monopsony
\end{enumerate}
\item What is meant by "economic resources" or "factorsof production"? Name three categories of
resources.
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item Capital, commodities, and currency
    \item raw materials, human resources and technology
    \item natural resources, artificial resources and human resources
    \item Water, oil, and minerals
    \item Intellectual property, workforce, and real estate
\end{enumerate}
\item Which of the following is NOT a feature of continuously compounded returns (i.e. log-
returns)?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item They can be interpreted as continuously compounded changes in the prices
    \item They can be added over time to give returns for longer time periods
    \item They can be added across a portfolio of assets to give portfolio returns
    \item They are usually fat-tailed
\end{enumerate}
\item Suppose transfer payments are greater than Social Security contributions corporate taxes
and retained earnings combined. In that case
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item NDP will be greater than GDP.
    \item NI will be greater than GDP.
    \item PI will be greater than NI.
    \item DPI will be greater than PI.
\end{enumerate}
\end{enumerate}
