\section*{Multiple Choice (10 marks)}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\arabic*.]
\item Which of the following is an accurate statement of the money supply in the United States?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item The money supply is backed by gold reserves.
    \item The least liquid measure of money is M2.
    \item M1 is larger than M3.
    \item The most liquid measure of money is M1.
\end{enumerate}
\item Which of the following statements about a price ceiling is accurate?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item An effective price ceiling must be at a price below the equilibrium price.
    \item A price ceiling will increase the quantity of the good supplied.
    \item A price ceiling will cause a shift in the demand curve for the good.
    \item A price ceiling will have no effect on the quantity of the good supplied.
\end{enumerate}
\item When the United States places an import quota on imported sugar we expect which of the
following effects?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item Consumers seek substitutes for sugar and products that use sugar.
    \item Consumers consume more sugar and products that use sugar.
    \item The supply of sugar increases.
    \item Net exports in the United States fall.
\end{enumerate}
\item Economists use the term "capital" to mean
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item money.
    \item plant and equipment.
    \item where the central government is located.
    \item the center of the economy.
\end{enumerate}
\item Which of the following is most likely to produce stronger economic growth over time?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item More rapid consumption of natural resources.
    \item Higher adult illiteracy rates.
    \item A falling stock of capital goods.
    \item Investment tax credits.
\end{enumerate}
\end{enumerate}
