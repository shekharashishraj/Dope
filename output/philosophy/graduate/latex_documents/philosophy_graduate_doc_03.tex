\section*{Multiple Choice (10 marks)}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\arabic*.]
\item Baxter argues that we should view our treatment of the environment as a matter of various
trade-offs whose aim is
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item to minimize damage incurred by all individual living beings.
    \item to promote the functional integrity of ecosystems.
    \item to minimize the suffering of all sentient creatures.
    \item to ensure the survival of endangered species.
    \item to minimize damage to the ozone layer.

\item In the context of Gardiner's article, "the fragmentation of agency" refers to what?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item an agent's inability to acknowledge his or her contribution to climate change
    \item the fragmentation of the effects of greenhouse gas emissions across generations
    \item the vast number of individuals and agents who contribute to climate change
    \item the idea that it's not rational for each individual to restrict his or her own pollution

\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item educational institutions.
    \item environmental entities.
    \item animals.
    \item governments.
    \item corporations.
\end{enumerate}
\item Arguing that someone couldn't have done something good because he is incapable of doing
good commits the fallacy of
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item Straw man
    \item Accident
    \item False dilemma
    \item Circular reasoning
    \item Red herring
\end{enumerate}

Walzer discusses?

    \item Terrorism is a necessary evil.
    \item Terrorism benefits oppressed groups.
    \item Terrorism is driven by economic factors.
    \item Terrorism is a legitimate form of self-defense.
\end{enumerate}
\end{enumerate}
