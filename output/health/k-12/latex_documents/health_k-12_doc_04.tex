\section*{Multiple Choice (10 marks)}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\arabic*.]
\item Assessing growth in routine practice should be:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item Height only
    \item Weight only
    \item Weight and height
    \item Head circumference
\end{enumerate}
\item The function of the gastrointestinal tract can be classified in several ways. Which of the
following functions is most important for survival immediately after a meal?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item Absorption and retention of water and electrolytes
    \item Elimination of toxins
    \item Maintenance of barrier function
    \item Extraction of maximum value from nutrients
\end{enumerate}
\item Which of the following is the yield of ATP from complete oxidation of glucose to carbon
dioxide and water?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item 10
    \item 25
    \item 30
    \item 32
\end{enumerate}
\item Which of the following statements is correct?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item Cholesterol mainly occurs in the cell walls of bacterial cells
    \item Cholesterol mainly occurs in the cell walls of mammalian cells
    \item Cholesterol mainly occurs in the cell walls of yeast cells
    \item Cholesterol mainly occurs in plant cell walls.
\end{enumerate}
\item Which of the following is a function of folate?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item Synthesis of TMP (thymidine monophosphate)
    \item Decarboxylation of amino acids to form amine neurotransmitters
    \item Synthesis of methionine from homocysteine
    \item Carboxylation of pyruvate to oxaloacetate
\end{enumerate}
\end{enumerate}
