\section*{Multiple Choice (10 marks)}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\arabic*.]
\item A piece of potato is dropped into a beaker of pure water. Which of the following describes the activity after the potato is immersed into the water?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item Water moves from the potato into the surrounding water.
    \item Water moves from the surrounding water into the potato.
    \item Potato cells plasmolyze.
    \item Solutes in the water move into the potato.
\end{enumerate}
\item A reaction that includes energy as one of its reactants is called a(n)
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item exergonic reaction.
    \item hydrolysis reaction.
    \item endergonic reaction.
    \item redox reaction.
\end{enumerate}
\item The resting membrane potential depends on which of the following? I. Active transport II. Selective permeability III. Differential distribution of ions...
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item III only
    \item I and II only
    \item II and III only
    \item I, II, and III
\end{enumerate}
\item The average birth weight for human babies is about 3 kg. Which of the following terms best describes this?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item artificial selection
    \item directional selection
    \item stabilizing selection
    \item disruptive selection
\end{enumerate}
\item In Darwin's thinking, the more closely related two different organisms are, the
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item more similar their habitats are.
    \item less similar their DNA sequences are.
    \item more recently they shared a common ancestor.
    \item less likely they are to have the same genes in common.
\end{enumerate}
\end{enumerate}

\section*{True / False (10 marks)}
\textit{Answer True or False and justify briefly.}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\arabic*.]
\item True or False: The correct answer to 'The sequence of amino acids in hemoglobin molecules of humans is more similar to the hemoglobin of chimpanzees than it is to the...' is 'humans and chimpanzees are more closely related than humans and dogs'.
\item True or False: The correct answer to 'Which is a true statement concerning genetic variation?' is 'It is created by the direct action of natural selection.'.
\item True or False: The correct answer to 'The primary site of glucose reabsorption is the' is 'loop of Henle'.
\item True or False: The correct answer to 'Which of the following statements about photosynthesis is incorrect?' is 'CO2 is an input to the Calvin cycle.'.
\item True or False: The correct answer to 'A heterotroph' is 'obtains its energy from sunlight, harnessed by pigments'.
\end{enumerate}
