\section*{Multiple Choice (10 marks)}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\arabic*.]
\item Ramanujan and Hardy played a game where they both picked a complex number. If the product
of their numbers was $32-8i$, and Hardy picked $5+3i$, what number did Ramanujan pick?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item 8-2i
    \item 4-i
    \item 5+3i
    \item 1+1i
    \item 7-5i
\end{enumerate}
\item Coloring the edges of a complete graph with n vertices in 2 colors (red and blue), what is
the smallest n that guarantees there is either a 4-clique in red or a 4-clique in blue?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item 12
    \item 15
    \item 14
    \item 16
    \item 22
\end{enumerate}
\item RS is the midsegment of trapezoid MNOP. If MN = 10x+3, RS=9x-1, and PO = 4x+7, what is the
length of RS?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item 20
    \item 30
    \item 31
    \item 34
    \item 32
\end{enumerate}
\item In how many ways can a group of 9 people be divided into 3 non-empty subsets?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item 2500
    \item 560
    \item 1512
    \item 4096
    \item 3025
\end{enumerate}
\item Find the smallest positive integer that leaves a remainder of 5 when divided by 8, a
remainder of 1 when divided by 3, and a remainder of 7 when divided by 11.
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item 103
    \item 100
    \item 123
    \item 300
    \item 315
\end{enumerate}
\end{enumerate}

\item True or False: The value of y that satisfies the equation y + 2.9 = 11 is 11.1.
\item True or False: In a complete graph with 22 vertices, it is guaranteed to find either a 4-clique in red or a 5-clique in blue.
\item True or False: The value of the integral \( \int_C \frac{dz}{z (z-2)^2} \) where \( C: |z - 2| = 1 \) is \( -0.3926 \).
\end{enumerate}
