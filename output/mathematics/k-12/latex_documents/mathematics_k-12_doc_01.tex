\section*{Multiple Choice (10 marks)}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\arabic*.]
\item If a freight train travels at a speed of 20 miles per hour for 6 hours, how far will it
travel?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item 120 miles
    \item 80 miles
    \item 26 miles
    \item 12 miles
\end{enumerate}
\item Stephanie flew 2,448 miles from Los Angeles to New York City. What is the number of miles
Stephanie flew rounded to the nearest thousand?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item 2,000
    \item 2,400
    \item 2,500
    \item 3,000
\end{enumerate}
\item Write an equation for the line tangent to the curve defined by F(t) = (t\^{}2 + 1, 2\^{}t) at
the point where y = 4.
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item y - 4 = ln 2(x - 2)
    \item y - 4 = 4 ln 2(x - 2)
    \item y - 4 = 4(x - 5)
    \item y - 4 = ln 2(x - 5)
\end{enumerate}
\item What is the shortest distance from the origin to the circle defined by $x^2-24x +y^2+10y
+160=0$?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item 10
    \item 16
    \item 24
    \item 12
\end{enumerate}
\item Keri spent 3 hours on her homework. She spent equal amounts of time on all subjects. If
Keri spent 1 over 2 hour on science, how many subjects did she study?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item 4 subjects
    \item 2 subjects
    \item 6 subjects
    \item 5 subjects
\end{enumerate}
\end{enumerate}
