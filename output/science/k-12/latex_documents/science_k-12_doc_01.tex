\section*{Multiple Choice (10 marks)}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\arabic*.]
\item Gavin has two rocks. Both rocks are made up entirely of the same mineral. What other
property of his two rocks is most likely to be the same?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item size
    \item shape
    \item color
    \item weight
\end{enumerate}
\item Which is an example of a human-made product?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item cake
    \item tomato
    \item wheat
    \item coal
\end{enumerate}
\item Sulfur (S), oxygen (O2), water (H2O), and sodium chloride (NaCl) are all examples of pure
substances. Which of the following describes all pure substances?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item A pure substance consists of only one type of element.
    \item A pure substance has a definite chemical composition.
    \item A pure substance cannot be broken down into simpler substances.
    \item A pure substance is normally found as a solid at room temperature.
\end{enumerate}
\item The speed of a sound wave varies as it travels through different substances. Which factor
will most affect the speed of a sound wave?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item the frequency of the wave
    \item the wavelength of the wave
    \item the source that created the sound
    \item the distance between molecules in the medium
\end{enumerate}
\item A lizard species mostly eats fruit from one particular type of tree. If a virus kills most
these trees, which individual lizards will most likely survive?
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item lizards that can climb higher in the fruit trees
    \item lizards that have darker coloration
    \item lizards that can find other types of food
    \item lizards that produce more offspring
\end{enumerate}
\end{enumerate}
